# Failure gallery — Chapter 5

Ten real wrong outputs from this group's own domain. Collect over the weeks before delivery.

Target mix:
- A fabricated citation, with the DOI check that exposed it
- A wrong coefficient in mechanics, concrete or fluids
- A confidently invented standard or code clause — **keep, but not among the ten the room grades together.** Design codes are not in the stated intersection of this cohort's knowledge, so a fabricated clause is checkable by some of the room and not all of it
- A unit or sign-convention error
- The interview-sourced claim about model "depression", presented alongside what happened when it was traced to a source

Each item needs: the prompt, the output, what is wrong, and how it was caught. The last field is the one that teaches.

**How to generate them, from `../../curriculum/ch02-annotation-findings.md` §11.** One vendor instructs contributors to aim prompts at a specific band of model knowledge — not common knowledge, where the model is reliable, and not genuinely obscure material, where it correctly declines, but the band between, where it believes it knows and confabulates. That band is where this group's own literature sits. Aim there rather than collecting failures opportunistically: it turns the gallery from an anecdote collection into a demonstration of a stated rule, and it explains why the room's own experience of models being reliable on textbook material and erratic on their research is not random.
